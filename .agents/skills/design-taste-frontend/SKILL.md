# Design Taste Frontend Skill (`design-taste-frontend`)

`design-taste-frontend` exists to ensure web applications and landing pages have genuine aesthetic taste, distinct personality, and world-class polish instead of looking like generic AI templates.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Taste

1. **One Cohesive System:**
   - Every interface must feel designed by a single confident human hand, not an assortment of copied UI components.
   - Choose one visual posture: either ultra-dense and technical (Linear, Raycast), warm and editorial (Stripe Press, ReadCV), or minimal and architectural (Vercel, Cosmos). Stick to it ruthlessly.

2. **Kill the Template Grids:**
   - Ban the predictable "Hero centered title + 3 equal feature cards with icons + pricing table" layout.
   - Use intentional asymmetry: 60/40 splits, large hero visual offset to one side, alternating horizontal rhythm.
   - Give hero sections physical presence: show the real product in action, an interactive canvas, or a high-density preview rather than generic abstract geometric blobs.

3. **Authentic Voice & Typography:**
   - Write crisp, confident copy without buzzword soup ("Unleash synergy with AI-powered next-gen solutions").
   - Pair type with intent: A distinctive display or serif font for titles paired with an ultra-clean neutral grotesque or mono for technical metadata.
   - Balance whitespace: Generous margin around key focal points, dense information packaging inside data components.

4. **Deliberate Moments & Tactile Polish:**
   - Every screen should have 1 or 2 "hero moments"—a delightfully responsive slider, a dynamic preview card, or a subtle canvas glow—rather than flashing effects everywhere.
   - Quiet micro-interactions: borders illuminate on hover, cards elevate by 1px with smooth transition, active tabs slide with fluid indicator lines.
   - Dark mode is not just inverted white: use deep rich tones (e.g., `#090A0F`, `#0D1117`), subtle 1px border highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and colored ambient backdrops.
